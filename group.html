Joining the group

PhD students: The department admits students and advisors are settled during the first year, so what matters is the departmental application, due December 1. Those drawn to quantitative biology can also apply to IQ Biology by November 15, a certificate taken alongside a departmental PhD. In either, say why you want to work with me and name others in the department. Emailing me first is not necessary and does not affect admission. If you do, read my research and publications pages, then send a CV and about 100 words on the problems you want to work on.

Postdoctoral researchers: I take on postdocs when I have funding for a particular project, and the department runs the Keith Julien Visiting Assistant Professorships on its own annual cycle. Both are advertised on the department site and on MathJobs; if nothing is posted, nothing is open. To name me as a potential advisor for a Julien VAP, check my research and publications pages, then write with your CV and a summary of the problems you would like to work on.

CU undergraduates and master’s students: I supervise one or two at a time, usually students who have done well in one of my courses. Check my research and publications pages, then write with the courses you have taken and the kind of problem you would like to work on.
