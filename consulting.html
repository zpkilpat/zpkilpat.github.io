WHO I WORK WITH

Clients

I work with research groups, companies, and public-sector organizations that have a decision or scientific question ordinary analysis has not answered. The models we build are mechanistic rather than black-box: their parameters stand for processes the system actually carries out, so a fit tells you why the data look the way they do, not only what comes next.

Organizations I have worked with include Consumer Reports, Workday, Dominion Energy, Seeq, FullContact, and the Allen Institute’s Mindscope Program, along with learners at government agencies and private banks. I teach data science for Data Society, and was a founding instructor of CU Boulder Tech Frontiers.

WORK I TAKE

Typical engagements

  • Fit a mechanistic model of how your users decide.
  • Diagnose why a group’s collective decisions go wrong.
  • Design an allocation rule that rewards what you intend.
  • Build a forecast from polling or market data.
  • Teach your team probabilistic thinking, one or two days.
