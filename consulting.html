Consulting & professional education

Mathematical modeling, decision science, and data science instruction for research groups, companies, and public-sector organizations.

AREAS OF EXPERTISE

Modeling, inference, and instruction

My consulting draws on research in Bayesian decision making, nonlinear dynamics, and stochastic processes, together with experience fitting mathematical models to data. I also develop and teach data science courses for professional audiences.

Organizations I have worked with include Consumer Reports, Workday, Dominion Energy, Seeq, FullContact, and the Allen Institute’s Mindscope Program, along with learners at government agencies and private banks. I teach data science for Data Society, and was a founding instructor of CU Boulder Tech Frontiers.

Decision & predictive modeling

Bayesian decision models, dynamical systems, and stochastic simulations—connected to data through model fitting and validation.

Model review & advisory

Examine assumptions, evaluate uncertainty, and connect mathematical theory to practical decisions and predictive performance.

Data science education

Tailored workshops for technical and mixed-background teams, from data science foundations to probabilistic thinking and scientific computing.

Engagements

Work can take the form of a focused model review, a scoped modeling and data-analysis project, or a customized workshop. Potential topics include sequential decision making, predictive models for dynamical systems, uncertainty quantification, and Bayesian methods.

Instruction can be designed for technical staff or broader professional audiences.

Advisory work is billed at $200–250 per hour. Scoped projects and workshops are quoted individually, based on the question, the preparation involved, and the deliverables.

I am glad to start with a free 20–30 minute call to work out whether I am the right person for the problem.
