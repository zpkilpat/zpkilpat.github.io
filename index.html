Zack Kilpatrick

Professor of Applied Mathematics
University of Colorado Boulder

My group studies the mathematical principles underlying cognition and collective behavior. We develop and analyze models of decision making, working memory, and foraging, with an emphasis on how individuals and groups use uncertain information in changing environments.

Our work combines Bayesian inference, stochastic processes, and nonlinear dynamics. We connect theory with behavioral and neural data to understand both the strategies used by biological systems and the mechanisms that implement them.

I received my PhD in Mathematics from the University of Utah in 2010 and held positions at the University of Pittsburgh and the University of Houston before joining CU Boulder in 2016.
